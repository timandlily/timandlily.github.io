Students install and operate four open-source security solutions — TheHive, Cortex, MISP, and Wazuh — configured to interoperate as a real SOC environment. Course covers CTI acquisition, sharing, and group-based incident simulation with virtual CERT and CTI teams collaborating on intrusion response.
