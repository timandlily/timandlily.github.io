## Overview
Introduction to the core concepts of intrusion response and cyber threat intelligence (CTI). Students will understand the relationship between incident response and threat intelligence in modern cybersecurity operations.

## Topics
- What is Cyber Threat Intelligence?
- Intrusion Detection vs Intrusion Prevention
- The SOC (Security Operations Center) concept
- CTI lifecycle: Planning, Collection, Processing, Analysis, Dissemination
- Introduction to the lab environment (TheHive, MISP, Wazuh)
